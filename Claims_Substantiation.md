**Open item:** Protect device count includes doorbells, chimes, sensors, viewports
and recorders, not just cameras. Filter Protect by device type in Site Manager to get
the true camera figure, then either raise the published number or keep 900+ if the
real count is near it. Do not round 1,142 up to "1,100+ cameras".

**Note:** 97 UniFi OS consoles against 51 published sites is plausible, since a site
can run more than one console, but confirm before anyone asks.
