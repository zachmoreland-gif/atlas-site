What we install

Everything a campus needs, on one platform.

Cameras that cover the whole campus

Worship spaces, lobbies, hallways, parking lots, and the perimeter, including PTZ cameras that read license plates in the dark. Footage stays on your own system, viewable by your staff from anywhere.

Intercom visitor screening

See and talk to whoever is at the door before it unlocks. Staff can answer from the office or their phone, and every visitor interaction is recorded.

Lockdown-ready door control

Every access-controlled door can be locked at once by the people you authorize, from a phone or a panic-style trigger. Doors unlock on schedule for services and lock themselves after.

PIN access for the school wing

Preschool and day-school parents get in with a PIN code, no cards to hand out and no app to install. Codes can change per family, per season, or the moment one needs to be shut off.

Real project

35 cameras. 10 doors. One weekend.

A 60,000 sq ft place of worship with a school on campus

A Kansas City-area congregation replaced its aging access-control and camera systems with a single UniFi platform: 35 G6 cameras including PTZ coverage for parking, 10 access-controlled doors, intercom visitor screening at the public entrances, PIN entry so school parents get in without a card or app, and lockdown-capable door control, all managed from one dashboard.

They needed eyes on the property fast. We worked through the weekend and had all 35 cameras up and recording in 48 hours, ready for Monday morning. The door retrofit followed over the next few days.
